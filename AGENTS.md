# Agent instructions

You cannot have AI as a co-author, or your contributions will be rejected.

This repository owns the responsibility stated in README.md. Use public pinned
contracts. Preserve dirty work and evidence. Scratch/logs go in ignored build/.
Run the installed common attribution validator and consumer conformance; do not
copy their implementation. Reserve shared heavy-check capacity. No implicit server,
benchmark, native/WASM build, history rewrite, remote enforcement change or deploy.
Never add AI author, committer or coauthor attribution to commits.

## Community-default ownership

This repository owns Earth's community documents and their public metadata.
The CI owner supplies executable attribution policy, shared templates and
conformance. Do not copy its checker here or change another owner's documents,
workflows, refs, locks or repository settings from this checkout.

Keep contributions in the repository that owns the behavior. Globe and image
behavior remains with the assigned Earth component; aircraft and campus content
remain with their hosts. New public modules require an explicit owning
repository and a recorded destination. Public references use documented exports
and exact releases; source maps do not replace preserved human authorship.

Scratch and retained evidence stay in this repository's ignored build tree.
Never remove evidence without the user's agreement. Preserve unrelated dirty
work. Documentation-only changes need no application build, typecheck or suite,
but their commit metadata always takes the same installed common checks.
Use the coordinator's check slot, keep logs and audit the complete actual
publication-ref scope; never qualify an empty history or synthetic-only scope.

Do not share personal data, accounts, credentials or material outside the task
with another service without consent. Retain license and factual tool notices;
contribution-credit advertising is prohibited under CONTRIBUTING's policy.
